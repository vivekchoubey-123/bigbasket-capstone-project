# BigBasket Data Story

## Dashboard story
The analysis covers January–June 2026 delivered revenue across six categories.

### Category status against target
- Household Essentials: Below Target - Watch
- Personal Care: Below Target - Watch
- Bakery: Below Target - Watch
- Dairy & Eggs: Below Target - Watch
- Snacks & Beverages: Below Target - Watch
- Fruits & Vegetables: Below Target - Watch

### Two recommendations
1. Prioritize Household Essentials with stronger inventory availability and supplier-volume negotiations because it is the largest revenue contributor.
2. Improve Fruits & Vegetables performance through assortment, freshness, and targeted monthly promotions because it is the lowest-revenue category.
